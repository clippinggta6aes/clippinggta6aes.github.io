Privacy Policy - GTA 6 Clips

Last updated: November 20, 2026

  1. Data collected. The app does not collect data from other users. It only stores the profile and access token of its publisher's own TikTok and Instagram accounts.
  2. Twitch chat data. It counts the number of messages in public Twitch chats to detect highlights. Message contents are not stored or shared.
  3. Storage. Videos and tokens stay on the publisher's own computer and private workspace. Nothing is sold or shared with third parties, except the platforms used for posting.
  4. Deletion. To remove content or data, write to lucas3aes3@gmail.com.
